Cheap folding camp kitchens rattle apart on the first corrugated road. The DOCK is different: a fully-equipped cooking station built inside a genuine Zarges® K470 expedition case — the same aluminium box that survives the world's toughest expeditions.

From box to kitchen

Set up in four simple moves

1

Place the closed kitchen on firm ground and lift off the platform with the legs.

2

Stand the kitchen on its platform — level and stable, wedge it if needed.

3

Fold out the bamboo worktop and clip on the stainless side shelves.

4

Set your stove on the top shelf and start cooking. That's it.

Frequently asked questions

Which box is the kitchen built into?

A genuine Zarges® K470 (model 40564) aircraft-aluminium case — the reference standard for expedition storage. It stacks and straps down like any of your other cases.

Is a stove or cookware included?

No — the kitchen is supplied empty so you can fit the stove and cookware you already love. There's dedicated space for a two-burner stove (up to 520 × 320 mm) and pans up to Ø 260 mm. We're happy to advise.

How much does it weigh and how big is it?

16.5 kg empty. Closed it measures 420 × 600 × 400 mm; set up, the worktop sits at a comfortable 810 mm height.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. DOCK kitchens are already in use on five continents and we arrange worldwide shipping. Message us with your location for details.

How do I order and pay?

There's no online checkout on purpose. You contact us via WhatsApp or e-mail, we confirm the details and the best way to pay and ship for your country. It's personal and transparent.

How long does it take?

Each kitchen is handbuilt to order. Lead time depends on the current queue — message us and we'll give you an honest estimate right away.
